# Saeid Jamali

My research spans operator algebras and dynamical systems, with ongoing interests
in interpretable learning, language-model evaluation, and the mathematical
foundations of reliable computation.

I study tracial regularity, group actions and crossed products of C*-algebras.
My computational research interests include learning a single interpretable hard
decision tree, evaluating probabilistic reasoning, and checking structured query
results exactly.
